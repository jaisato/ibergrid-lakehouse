Spain–Portugal Spread, Decoupling & 2025-04-28 Iberian Blackout Spotlight

On 28 April 2025, the Iberian Peninsula experienced a historic grid blackout: peninsular demand plunged ~28 % day-over-day alongside decoupled Spain/Portugal prices. Cross-source validation against REData spot on decoupled hours prevents silent ES/PT column swaps (ADR 0004 & ADR 0008).

Separated Spanish Electric Systems (Peninsular vs Non-Peninsular)

Non-peninsular systems (Baleares, Canarias, Ceuta, Melilla) do not clear on the peninsular MIBEL day-ahead market and are never mixed into peninsular totals (ADR 0003 & ADR 0007).

Canarias timestamp integrity flag (ADR 0003): REData stamps Canarias hourly points with peninsular UTC offsets while load shapes indicate Canarian wall-clock time. Silver and Gold carry ts_utc_verified = FALSE on every Canarias row so cross-system timestamp joins cannot occur silently.
